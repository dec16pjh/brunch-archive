(Perfomamance)

I'm a tree, in 21th century.  After human's developement of  
finding we have soul and our own language and machine of  
translating it. I'm the first one to have a chance to talk to you,  
human.  
We have long history, still longer than you. from near to the  
time of being lives in this planet, we had been. Exactly to say,  
we were the first lives in this planet. After long time from the  
time we created, the human appeared in this planet.  
Not only the human but also other lives in earth were served the  
oxygen that we made to survive. Without our help, no living thing  
could not exist.  
At first, human respected and feared the other natures. The first  
human-being knew that every creation has soul. of course in that  
time, man was the one of the weakest creations. they were not  
enough strong and could not run fast. to climb and hide into our  
body was all what they could do to escape from the hungry beasts.  
they slept on our breaches and ate our fruits.  
But they could use their hands and they were smart. Living in  
groups, slowly they had found the way to guard themselves and to  
govern other creations. After the dull Prometheus giving the fire  
to man, man became overbearing, insolent. they forgot the way to  
conversation with other natures. In fact, they didn't want to  
listen to them. they started killing other creatures including us,  
for the sake of themselves

they cut our bodies to make their house and to use them for the  
source of fire. Still at that time, we were painful but happy to  
give you the house and warmth.  
as the time passed, the human's science got stronger and stronger.  
they forgot even the feeling of thanking to us. they forgot the  
value of our exist. many factory were founded and the car and  
airplane took the place of horses. their shit, oops i'm sorry,  
their smoke choked us. The death water out of the factories  
killed our families. Now the human ruin the wood not for their  
house, they did for money. Even now the human's egoism ruins  
other natures, not only us.  
we are the reason of that you can be. we were your eldest brother  
and the first son of the earth that you would call mother. we  
have made you survive by our breath, we have made you more smart  
by giving you the paper made of our body. we were the only one on  
your side, we were your house, we were your food. But you forgot  
your brothers and betrayed your mother. how could and can you do  
that, our youngest brother?  
you kill the other lives not for your life. Though you pretend to  
have moral, I don't think so. No others except man kill other  
alive things not for survive. do you want convenient life? if so,  
give up the moral. do you want to live in plenty? do, if you can  
do it without killing other lives.  
why do you respect other human's life? you, smart guy, will say  
that is because you also have the life and you know how it is  
valuable. In the same way, if you respect other human's life, you  
have to respect other nature's lives. because they also have  
lives endowed from god just like yours.  (1999)
